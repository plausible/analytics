        Rules for who is allowed to change which team roles.

        A role change is allowed when the actor may modify the member or invitation in its current role (can_modify_member?/2, can_modify_invitation?/2), and may assign the new role (can_assign?/2). The ensure_* functions combine both for the services that make the change, and deny anyone who isn't a member of the team.

        Rules that depend on the team's state rather than on who's asking (the last owner can't be demoted, owners need 2FA when the team enforces it, etc) should stay in the services that make the change.
