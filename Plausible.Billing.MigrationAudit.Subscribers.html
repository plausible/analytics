        Part of the Paddle Billing migration audit fetched from local db. How subscribers are distributed by status, what they effectively pay, and how enterprise (customer-specific) plans look. Read-only, and all output is aggregated, no customer identifiers.

        audit(opts \\ [])

        @spec audit(Keyword.t()) :: map()

        Returns a map with the following keys:

        • :statuses - the current subscription of every team, by status. Cancelled subscriptions are split into those still inside the paid period and those that expired. :teams_on_app_trial are teams in our own trial without any subscription
        • :billed - live subscriptions (active, past due, paused, or cancelled but paid up) by kind, currency and interval: the effective amount paid.
        • :vs_list_price - for regular plans, how the amount paid compares to the list price in plan_prices.json, by currency. The number is compared as is, so for EUR and GBP "equal" means parity with the USD price. Anything that isn't equal is a discount, a modifier, a negotiated price or a grandfathered one. :off_list_price_points lists the most common ones.
        • :enterprise - live enterprise plans: price points (distinct currency, interval and amount combinations), by interval, and how many plans are shared or repeated.

        Options:

        • :today - date for "inside the paid period" (default: today, UTC).
        • :list_prices - map of plan id => USD list price (default: plan_prices.json from priv).
