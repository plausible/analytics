        Service for moving sites between teams.

        There are two ways for a transfer to happen:

        1. The email path: initiate/3 -> accept/4

        An owner or admin of the site's team starts an ownership transfer which sends out an invitation email. The invitee then chooses whether to accept the transfer with or without "members" (guests).

        If they accept with members:

        • the site keeps its guests and pending guest invitations
        • the owners of the prior team become guest editors of the site

        If they accept without members:

        • all guest memberships/invitations of the site are dropped
        • the owners of the prior team do not keep guest access

        2. A single action: change_team/3

        A user moves the site directly to another team that they manage.

        • the site keeps its guests and pending guest invitations
        • the owners of the prior team become guest editors of the site

        Creates a site transfer to the given email and sends the invitee an email to accept it. Only team owners and admins can do this.

        If a transfer of this site to this email already exists, the email is sent again with the existing transfer link, and the transfer's updated_at is set to now. The transfer keeps its original expiry.

        Rejects if there's an existing guest invitation (not transfer) for this site and this email.
