        Tooling for migrating from Paddle Classic to Paddle Billing.

        • Products reflect plan definitions from JSON files 1:1
        • Prices for each product reflect plans from Paddle Classic
        • Enterprise plans are linked to a single product, each with a custom price
        • Legacy classic plans with a single currency are mapped to a respective price accordingly, without overrides

        sync_enterprise(overwrite? \\ false)

        Creates a custom product and respective price for every enterprise plan in the database that doesn't have one in Paddle Billing yet.

        Enterprise plans are always single currency, but the database doesn't record which one. The currency and the amount are read from the plan in Paddle Classic, by paddle_plan_id. Plans that don't have exactly one recurring price raise before anything is written to Paddle Billing.

        Plans without a Classic counterpart are skipped and their ids are printed. These are manual subscriptions and plans deleted from Classic. Duplicates are skipped too: teams sharing a paddle_plan_id share its price.

        With overwrite? set, existing prices (and the product) are updated as well.

        Returns the mapping of every Classic plan id to its Billing product and price ids, like sync_standard/1.

        sync_standard(overwrite? \\ false, canonical_plans \\ Plans.all())

        Creates the products and prices for the standard plans that don't exist in Paddle Billing yet.

        With overwrite? set, the plans that already exist are updated as well: their products and prices are patched to match the current plan definitions, and prices that are missing are created.

        Returns the mapping of every Classic plan id to its Billing product and price ids, whether they were created now or found in Paddle, so repeated runs return the same mapping.

        The generation of a plan is only put in the price description, which is internal. Product and price names are displayed to customers.

        update_plan_files(dir \\ priv_dir(), plan_files \\ ["legacy_plans", "plans_v1", "plans_v2", "plans_v3", "plans_v4", "plans_v5"])

        Inlines Billing IDs of standard plans into static plan definitions (priv/plans_v*.json).

        Assumes migration was run already. Every plan gets billing_product_id, billing_monthly_price_id and billing_yearly_price_id, read from Paddle Billing.

        Plans are linked to them through their Classic ids, stored in custom_data.

        update_sandbox_plan_files(dir \\ priv_dir(), plan_files \\ ["legacy_plans", "plans_v1", "plans_v2", "plans_v3", "plans_v4", "plans_v5"])

        Like update_plan_files/2, but for sandbox plan definitions (priv/sandbox_*.json).

        Sandbox plans have their own Classic ids, so they are matched to the "live" plan by the same generation, kind and monthly pageview limit, so we can keep using Billing Sandbox on both environments (prod and staging) for the time being.
